# Conduct

This project exists to specify and build Linux engineering tools. Keep it on that work.

**Required:**

- Stay on the engineering topic of the issue, spec, or patch.
- No harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- No proprietary intellectual property, leaked material, or NDA content.
- Disagree about specs, tests, and patches — not about people.

**Moderation:**

Maintainers may edit, lock, close, or restrict participation when these rules are broken.

Off-topic threads get closed. Political, ideological, and identity debate does not belong here.

Full project process: [community](https://github.com/linux-engineering-tools/community).
